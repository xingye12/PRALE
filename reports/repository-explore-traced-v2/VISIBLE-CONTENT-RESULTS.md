# Bash/read-only returned-content rescore

Only bash-returned source evidence and successful read content contribute. Explorer, edit, write, filename listings and internal scans do not contribute. Paths are deduplicated per task. Bash attribution remains heuristic/conservative, not guaranteed exhaustive. No provider rerun.
